Course Map

How each chapter maps to the Product Management 360 certificate courses.

This handbook is a companion text for the topics in the Product Management 360 certificate. It is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Cornell University or eCornell. The chapters cover each course topic at the level of product decisions. The data courses (spreadsheets, SQL, visualization) are covered in Part IV the way a product leader works in 2026: by asking the right questions of an AI harness, not by learning syntax.
